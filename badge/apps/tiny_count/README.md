# Tiny Count

Tiny Count is a gentle counting game for toddlers where every touch adds one friendly object and the big number.

## How to play

- **Tap & Count** (the default): touch any pad and one object drops into the grid
  while the big number goes up by one. The objects sit in rows of five, so a
  grown-up can point and count together: "One, two, three!" At 5, 10 and 15 the
  objects do a happy wiggle and a case light glows; at 20 there is a confetti
  celebration, then the objects float away and a new theme (apples, stars,
  fish, balloons, ducks, flowers, ladybugs) begins. Shaking the badge gives the
  objects a gentle jiggle.
- **Number Book**: pages 1 to 20. **RIGHT** or **DOWN** turns to the next page,
  **LEFT** or **UP** to the previous. Each page counts itself up, one object at
  a time, with the number. Any other pad plays the count again.
- The four case lights fill like a progress bar: one more light for every five
  objects.
- Presses are counted at most once every 0.3 seconds, however many pads are
  touched, so mashing never jumps the count. If nobody touches the badge for
  90 seconds in Tap & Count, the count quietly starts again from 0.

## For grown-ups

- **Switch mode**: hold **BACK** for 3.5 seconds. A small ring fills in the
  bottom-right corner, then two big picture tiles appear (a hand with a "1",
  and an open book). **LEFT**/**RIGHT** choose, **SELECT** confirms. With no
  input for 15 seconds the picker closes and play continues. A quick tap of
  BACK is just a toddler touch.
- **HOME** returns to the badge menu. Toddlers may touch it by accident; just
  open Tiny Count again.
- The app always opens in Tap & Count; the picker remembers the last mode you
  chose, and each launch starts with a new theme.
- There is no score, no timer, no way to lose and no text apart from numerals.
- **No audio**: the badge has no speaker, so numbers are visual. Say the
  numbers out loud as you count together.
- Aimed at ages 18 months to 3 years, played together with a grown-up. Always
  supervise play: the badge is small, has a battery, and the lanyard is not a
  toy.

## Hardware

Uses the touch pads, the motion sensor (shake) and the four case lights. No
Wi-Fi or `secrets.py` needed. Shake and lights need a physical badge.
